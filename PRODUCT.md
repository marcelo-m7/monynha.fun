# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Curators, learners, and culture-focused community members who want to discover, preserve, and contextualize meaningful videos that algorithmic feeds usually bury. They use Tube O2 in a task-oriented flow: discover, evaluate, save, organize into playlists, discuss, and share.

## Product Purpose
Tube O2 exists to make human editorial judgment the primary ranking signal for cultural video discovery. The product helps people preserve high-value content, build learning paths, and sustain community discussion with clear context and trust. Success means users can reliably find quality videos faster, understand why they matter, and contribute curation without fighting noisy platform mechanics.

## Brand Personality
Bold, Futuristic, Playful.
Voice and tone should feel energetic and technologically sharp while remaining clear and practical for everyday curation tasks. The emotional target is confidence, momentum, and curiosity.

## Anti-references
- Traditional corporate enterprise UX aesthetics (for example, SAP/Oracle/Microsoft 365 style visual language).
- Minimal static website aesthetics with low motion and low visual personality.
- Generic blog-like or business-dashboard visual identity that weakens product distinctiveness.

## Design Principles
- Human editorial intelligence first: every interface should reinforce that people curate meaning, while automation assists in the background.
- Motion with intent and speed: dynamic interactions should communicate state and momentum, never decoration-only spectacle.
- Structural clarity at high energy: geometric, grid-driven layouts must remain scannable under real product workloads.
- Consistent premium interaction model: shared component behavior, transitions, and feedback should feel cohesive across routes.
- Performance as a product feature: rich visuals must preserve responsiveness and smoothness across desktop and mobile contexts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum standard across all key flows and surfaces.
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations while keeping a strong default motion system for users who have not opted out.
Maintain keyboard operability, visible focus states, and robust color contrast in both light and dark modes.
